# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

Project discussions should be respectful, focused, and constructive. Examples of expected behavior include:

- using welcoming and inclusive language
- accepting different viewpoints and experience levels
- giving feedback that is specific and actionable
- focusing criticism on code, documentation, and project decisions

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discriminatory language, and personal attacks.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or block participants who do not follow these standards.
